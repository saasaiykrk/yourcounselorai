# YourCounselor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

YourCounselor ("we", "us") is operated by Fabeminds, 5/12, Vijay Vilas, Anandnagar, GB Road, Thane (MS), India. This policy explains what the YourCounselor app, website and booking pages collect, why, and your choices. YourCounselor is a clinical reference and documentation aid for registered mental-health professionals. It is not intended for patients, the public or anyone under 18.

## 1. Information we collect

**From clinicians (app users)**
- Account details: email address, mobile number, name, gender, age, professional role, registration body and registration number, used to verify that you are a registered professional and to contact you. If you start creating an account and do not enter the email code, the details you entered are kept for 90 days so we can help you finish, then deleted.
- Case text you write: before anything leaves your phone, the app removes names, phone numbers, email addresses, ID numbers and similar identifiers, and you confirm the text contains none. Our server checks again and refuses text with identifiers. We store de-identified case text and the replies so you can see them in History.
- Payments for plans, single reports and single prints of education pages: plan, amount and payment status. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay; we never see or store them.
- Your own AI provider API key, if you choose to add one: stored encrypted, never shown again.
- Safety reports you send us about a reply.

**From clients who book an appointment** through a clinician's booking page
- First name, email address and the appointment time. Nothing else is asked.
- These are stored encrypted, used only to manage that appointment and send its emails, never sent to an AI provider, and deleted 90 days after the appointment.

**What we do not collect**: no location, contacts, photos, advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting data. The app shows no advertising.

## 2. How we use it
This section covers the information in section 1. **Google user data (from connecting Google Calendar) is not used for any purpose in this section;** it is used only to provide the Google Calendar and Meet feature, as described in section 4.

- To verify registration and provide the service.
- To generate consultation replies and reports from de-identified case text.
- To show your name and email as a watermark on education pages you open.
- To check every reply for clinical safety before it is shown.
- To process payments and keep records required by law.
- To manage appointments and send booking emails.
- To review safety reports and improve safety.

- To send you offers, news and updates about YourCounselor at the mobile number and email address you gave when you created your YourCounselor account (WhatsApp, SMS, email or a call). We never use the email address or any other data of a Google account you connect for this. Reply STOP to any message, or email us, to stop them at any time.

We do not sell personal data. We do not show advertising in the app, and case information is never used for marketing.

## 4. Google user data (Google Calendar and Google Meet)
This applies only to clinicians who choose to connect their Google account in the app (Calendar → Booking settings → Connect Google Calendar).

- **What we access:** permission to manage events on your calendar (the `calendar.events` scope), and your Google account's email address, only to show you in the app which account is connected.
- **Only for this feature:** we use Google user data only to provide this user-facing feature: putting your YourCounselor appointments on your Google Calendar with a Meet link. We do not use it for marketing, offers or newsletters, advertising, profiling, analytics, credit or lending decisions, or to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models, and it is never combined with your other YourCounselor data for any of these purposes.
- **How we use it:** only to create, update and delete Google Calendar events, with a Google Meet link, for appointments booked through YourCounselor, on your own calendar, and to invite the client to that event. When an appointment is rescheduled we update the same event; when it is cancelled or declined we delete it. We do not read, list or change any other events on your calendar.
- **What goes to Google:** the appointment time, your display name, the session type and the client's email address. No clinical information is ever sent to Google.
- **Storage and security:** the permission Google gives us (a refresh token) is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on our server, is never shown to anyone and is used only for the purpose above. Each event's ID and Meet link are stored with the appointment.
- **Sharing:** we do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising, do not use it to train AI models, never send it to an AI provider, and do not transfer it to anyone except as needed to provide this feature, for security, or when the law requires it. No one at YourCounselor reads it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
- **Limited Use:** YourCounselor's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
- **Your choice:** you can disconnect at any time in the app (Calendar → Booking settings → Disconnect), which revokes our access at Google; you can also remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting your YourCounselor account deletes the stored permission and revokes it. Events already on your calendar stay in your Google Calendar until you delete them.

## 5. Security
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Clients' booking details and API keys are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
- On the phone, case text is kept in memory only and never saved. Screenshots are blocked on case screens, and app backups are off.
- Administrator access to consults is limited to safety review, and every access is recorded.

## 6. How long we keep it
- De-identified consults: 12 months, unless linked to an open safety report.
- Appointment records, including client name and email: 90 days after the appointment.
- Payment records: as long as tax law requires.
- Account details: until you delete your account.
- Google Calendar permission: until you disconnect or delete your account.

## 7. Your rights and choices
Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:
- ask to see or correct your data;
- ask us to delete your account and data;
- withdraw consent;
- nominate someone to act for you.

To delete your account, use **Account → Delete account** in the app, or follow the steps at https://app.yourcounselor.in/delete-account (which also gives an email address if you can no longer open the app). Clients can cancel a booking using the link in their confirmation, or email us to have their details deleted.

## 8. Children
YourCounselor is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data from app users.
