## Full description (max 4,000 characters)
YourCounselor is a clinical reference and documentation aid for registered psychologists, psychiatrists and counselling professionals in India.

Describe a case in your own words and get a structured clinical work-up: case history and mental status audit, safety and risk considerations, differential considerations with ICD-11 codes checked against the World Health Organization's ICD-11, assessment tools, and a session plan.

GUIDED CONSULTATION
Start with a short case description. The app asks only the questions the report needs, one at a time, explains why each matters, and lets you answer "don't know" or skip. Then it writes a complete consultation report you can review and download as a PDF.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Names, phone numbers, email addresses and ID numbers are removed on your phone before anything is sent, and you confirm the case contains no identifiers.
• Case text is never saved on your phone. Screenshots are blocked on case screens.
• The server checks again and refuses any text with identifiers.
• No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.

SAFETY CHECKED
Every reply passes an automatic clinical safety check before it reaches you. Replies that fail are held back, never shown. Crisis helplines for India are always one tap away.

APPOINTMENTS
Share your booking link. Clients choose a free time, and you approve or confirm automatically. Set your working hours, breaks, appointment length and days off. Clients give only a first name and email.

YOUR HISTORY
Search, label and revisit your past consults. Only you can see them.

KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY
Free for every user, in sections (Basics, Therapies, Practice, Others, Handouts): therapy modules (CBT, ACT, DBT, Behavioural Activation, Motivational Interviewing, Exposure Therapy and more), a therapy comparison chart, a protocol matrix and glossary, and a case history and MSE practice handout. Pages open inside the app. Print or save any page as a PDF with a subscription, or unlock a single page for 24 hours.

PLANS
Monthly plans with 30 or 50 reports, single reports, or your own AI provider key. Payments are handled securely by Razorpay.

WHO IT IS FOR
YourCounselor is for registered mental-health professionals only. Every account is checked against professional registration (for example, RCI) before use.

IMPORTANT
YourCounselor supports your clinical judgement; it does not replace it. It is not a diagnostic device and is not intended for use by patients or the public. You remain responsible for every clinical decision. In an emergency, contact local emergency services (112 in India).
