# Mode templates — copy the skeleton for the chosen mode and fill every heading
Headings and numbers are fixed. A section may say "Not applicable — [reason]" but is never dropped. `{…}` = fill in. If the confidence ceiling is Low, prefix every heading from Section 3 onward with **PROVISIONAL — Low confidence ·**.
