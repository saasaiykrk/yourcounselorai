# Documentation templates (v2.1 Mode F — Documentation Support)
Always start with a one-line **safety check** ("Risk reviewed this session: yes/no; status: …"). Anonymise. Keep facts, observations and interpretations distinct. Flag any statement not supported by recorded history or MSE. End with the v2.1 mandatory disclaimer only when it is a draft for the clinician (not inside the final clinical record).

## 1. SOAP progress note
- **S — Subjective:** client's report since last session, in brief; key quotes; homework completed or not
- **O — Objective:** observations (appearance, affect, engagement); scores with dates (e.g., PHQ-9 = 12, down from 17)
- **A — Assessment:** clinical impression; progress against each goal; current risk level with basis
- **P — Plan:** next session focus; homework set; referrals or coordination; next appointment
*Footer:* date · duration · format (in person / online) · clinician and level

## 2. DAP note
- **D — Data:** what was said and observed, including scores
- **A — Assessment:** interpretation and progress
- **P — Plan:** next steps and homework

## 3. Intake summary
Identifying details (anonymised) · referral source and question · presenting problem (onset, duration, impact) · relevant history · safety screen · MSE summary (Core 1 ASM-05) · provisional 5 Ps formulation · initial plan and measures

## 4. Risk assessment note (Core 1 ASM-06)
Trigger for assessment · ideation / plan / intent / means / past attempts · protective factors · risk level (low / moderate / high) with basis · actions taken (safety plan, means restriction, family involvement with consent, referral) · crisis numbers given · supervisor informed (yes/no) · review date

## 5. Treatment plan (short form)
Problem · ICD-11 considerations (to confirm) · long-term goal · objectives with target / unit / setting / deadline (Core 3 TPE-02) · linked interventions with evidence grade · measures and review dates

## 6. Psychological assessment report
Use Core 6 RPT-01 (structure), RPT-02 (writing rules) and run the RPT-03 checklist before handing over. Number the referral questions and answer each one.

## 7. Referral letter
To · From (name, qualification, RCI number) · Date · Client (initials, age) · **Reason for referral and urgency** · Relevant background (brief) · Current risk status · What has been tried and the response · Specific question for the recipient · Consent to share obtained (yes / date) · Contact for follow-up

## 8. Discharge / closing summary
Dates of treatment and number of sessions · goals and outcomes (baseline vs final scores, reliable change — Core 6 BRF) · relapse-prevention plan · follow-up or booster arrangements · open issues and referrals
